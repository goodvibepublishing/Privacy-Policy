Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 1, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This app is designed to keep your personal journaling safe and private.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information to external servers.

The app may ask for your name to personalize your experience. This information, along with your journal entries, streaks, rewards, and preferences, is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

All journal entries and related app data remain on your device.

2. Notifications

The app may request permission to send daily journaling reminders. These notifications are generated locally on your device.

We do not use external servers or third-party push notification services.

3. Background Audio

The app includes optional background sounds to enhance your journaling experience. These audio files are bundled within the app and do not require data collection or external streaming.

4. Use of Your Data

Since all data stays on your device, we do not access, view, collect, analyze, or process your journal entries, name, streaks, or rewards in any way.

5. Third-Party Services

This app does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party services that collect user data.

6. Security

Your data is stored locally on your device. We recommend using your device’s built-in security features (such as a passcode or biometric authentication) to protect your information.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change how data is handled (for example, if we introduce cloud syncing or analytics), we will update this Privacy Policy and reflect the updated “Last Updated” date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: goodvibepublishing@gmail.com
