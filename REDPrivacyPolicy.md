# Privacy Policy for Real Emotion Detector

**Last Updated:** [2/21/26]

## Introduction

Real Emotion Detector ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application ("App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Photos and Images
- **What we collect:** When you choose to analyze a photo, the App accesses the photo you select from your device's photo library.
- **How we collect it:** You manually select photos from your gallery. The App does not automatically access your photos.
- **When we collect it:** Only when you explicitly choose a photo to analyze emotions.

### No Personal Information
The App does not collect, store, or transmit:
- Your name, email address, or contact information
- Location data
- Device identifiers
- Usage analytics or tracking data
- Any other personally identifiable information

## How We Use Your Information

### On-Device Processing Only
- **Emotion Analysis:** Selected photos are processed entirely on your device using on-device machine learning models.
- **No Upload:** Photos are never uploaded to any server, cloud service, or third-party service.
- **Temporary Processing:** Photos are processed in memory and are not permanently stored by the App.

### Machine Learning Models
The App uses:
- **TFLite Models:** On-device TensorFlow Lite models for emotion classification
- **Google ML Kit:** On-device face detection (runs locally on your device)
- All processing occurs locally; no data is sent to Google or any other service

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage Only
- All processing happens on your device
- Photos are not stored by the App after processing
- Analysis results are not permanently saved
- No data is stored in the cloud or on external servers

### Security Measures
- All processing is performed locally on your device
- No network transmission of your photos or data
- No external data storage

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### No Third-Party Sharing
We do not share, sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to third parties. Specifically:
- **No cloud services:** Your photos are never uploaded to cloud storage
- **No analytics:** We do not use analytics or tracking services
- **No advertising:** The App does not display ads or share data with advertisers
- **No social media:** We do not integrate with social media platforms

### Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information only if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

## Permissions

### Photo Library Access
- **Purpose:** To allow you to select photos from your gallery for emotion analysis
- **Scope:** The App only accesses photos you explicitly choose to analyze
- **Automatic Access:** The App does not automatically access your entire photo library

### Camera Permission
- **Status:** Not used. The App does not access your device's camera.

## Your Rights and Choices

### Control Over Your Data
- You choose which photos to analyze
- You can stop using the App at any time
- No account or registration required
- You can delete the App at any time, which removes all local data

### Data Access and Deletion
Since we do not collect or store your data externally:
- No data to access or delete from our servers (we don't have any)
- All processing happens locally on your device
- Deleting the App removes all local processing data

## Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can delete such information.

## Third-Party Services

- **Purpose:** On-device face detection
- **Data Processing:** All processing occurs locally on your device
- **Data Transmission:** No data is sent to Google servers
- **Privacy:** Google ML Kit's on-device processing does not transmit your photos or data

### TensorFlow Lite
- **Purpose:** On-device emotion classification
- **Data Processing:** All processing occurs locally on your device
- **Data Transmission:** No data is transmitted externally
